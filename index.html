Simple rules. Real stakes.

Keep your streaks alive and restore your knight's health. Stay consistent and watch your knight level up.

Questions

Is Checkpoint free?

Checkpoint is free to download, and you can start your first quest right away. Some extra features may be offered as optional in-app purchases.

What happens when my HP runs out?

Missed days cost HP, and completed quests slowly heal your knight. Keep checking in to stay in the game — even one quest a day helps.

Do I need an account?

No. Checkpoint works without sign-up and keeps your data on your iPhone.

How many habits should I start with?

Start small. A few minutes a day is enough. One or two quests you can actually keep beat ten you'll abandon.
