Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Checkpoint ("the app") is a habit tracker for iPhone. This policy explains what happens with your data when you use it.

Data we collect

We do not collect any personal data. Checkpoint has no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics and no advertising.

Data stored on your device

The habits you create, your check-ins, streaks and your knight's progress (level, XP, HP) are stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to third parties.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. You can turn them off at any time in iOS Settings.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual habits in the app, or remove all data by deleting the app from your device.

Children

Checkpoint does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
