Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

The short version

Checkpoint has no registration, no ads and no analytics or tracking. We don't collect your name, email or phone number. Your gameplay data is synced to the cloud under an anonymous identifier for backup and the Hall of Legends leaderboard. We don't sell or share it.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Checkpoint mobile application (the "App") handles information. The App is developed and published by an independent developer ("we", "us", "our"). By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

1. Information we collect

We do not require registration and do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address or phone number.

To enable cloud synchronization, backup and the global Hall of Legends leaderboard, the App stores anonymous gameplay data linked to an anonymous user identifier. This data includes:

  • the habits you create (name, icon, color, reminder time);
  • your check-ins, streaks and history;
  • your knight's progress, such as level, HP, XP, dungeon progress and its randomly generated name.

The anonymous identifier is created automatically by the App and is not linked to your real identity. We do not use third-party advertising, analytics or tracking tools.

2. How your data is stored

The App keeps a copy of your data on your device and syncs it to Google Firebase, a cloud service provided by Google. Firebase stores the data on our behalf and may process technical information needed to deliver the service, such as your IP address, under Firebase's privacy and security terms. Data sent between the App and Firebase is encrypted in transit.

Depending on your device settings, on-device data may also be included in your device's own backups (for example, iCloud backups), which are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

3. Hall of Legends leaderboard

The global Hall of Legends leaderboard shows your knight's name and progress (such as level) to other players. It does not show your habits, your real name or any contact details.

4. Reminders and notifications

If you set a reminder time for a habit, the App schedules local notifications on your device. Notifications are created and delivered by your device's operating system; no information is sent to a server. You can allow or disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

5. In-app purchases and subscriptions

The App may offer optional in-app purchases or subscriptions. All payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details, Apple ID or billing address. Apple may share with us only anonymous, aggregated sales information. Purchases are handled under Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties. The only service provider that processes it is Google Firebase, which stores it on our behalf as described above. Your knight's name and progress are visible to other players on the leaderboard.

7. Data retention and deletion

We keep your gameplay data for as long as it is needed to provide sync, backup and the leaderboard. You can delete individual habits inside the App. Deleting the App removes data from your device but does not by itself delete the copy stored in the cloud. To have your cloud data deleted, contact us at the email below and we will remove the data linked to your anonymous identifier.

8. Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). Gameplay data is stored only under an anonymous identifier.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your data. You can view and edit your data directly in the App. To request deletion of your cloud data or ask any other question, contact us using the details below.

10. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. These providers may log technical data such as your IP address as part of delivering the page, under their own privacy policies. We do not use cookies or analytics on this website.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time, for example if new features are added to the App. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a change means the App starts collecting personally identifiable information, we will update this Policy before that change takes effect.

12. Contact

Questions about this Policy? Email us at checkpoint.todo@gmail.com.
