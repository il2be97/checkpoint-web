Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

The short version

Checkpoint has no accounts, no servers, no analytics and no ads. Your habits and progress stay on your device. We can't see them, and we don't sell or share anything.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Checkpoint mobile application (the "App") handles information. The App is developed and published by an independent developer ("we", "us", "our"). By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, receive or store any personal information. The App does not require registration, does not ask for your name, email or phone number, and does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

2. Information stored on your device

To work, the App saves the following data locally on your device:

  • the habits you create (name, icon, color, reminder time);
  • your check-ins, streaks and history;
  • your in-game character's progress (such as level, experience and health) and its randomly generated name.

This data is stored only in the App's private storage on your device. It is not transmitted to us or to any third party. Depending on your device settings, it may be included in your device's own backups (for example, iCloud or iTunes backups), which are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

3. Reminders and notifications

If you set a reminder time for a habit, the App schedules local notifications on your device. Notifications are created and delivered by your device's operating system; no information is sent to a server. You can allow or disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

4. In-app purchases and subscriptions

The App may offer optional in-app purchases or subscriptions. All payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details, Apple ID or billing address. Apple may share with us only anonymous, aggregated sales information. Purchases are handled under Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Sharing of information

Because we do not collect personal information, we have nothing to sell, rent or share with third parties.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device for as long as you keep the App. You can delete individual habits inside the App, reset all data in Settings, or delete the App to permanently remove all of its data from your device.

7. Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). Since no personal data is collected, no data about children is collected either.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data. Since all App data is stored only on your device, you can exercise these rights directly in the App or by deleting it. If you have questions, contact us using the details below.

9. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. These providers may log technical data such as your IP address as part of delivering the page, under their own privacy policies. We do not use cookies or analytics on this website.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time, for example if new features are added to the App. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a change means the App starts collecting personal information, we will update this Policy before that change takes effect.

11. Contact

Questions about this Policy? Email us at checkpoint.todo@gmail.com.
