Common questions

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Checkpoint, go to the purchase screen and tap “Restore Purchases”. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Checkpoint, then tap Cancel Subscription. You keep access until the end of the current period.

How do I request a refund?

All payments are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I got a new phone. Can I move my progress?

Your habits and progress are kept on your device and backed up to the cloud under an anonymous identifier. If you restore your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one, your habits and progress come with it. If something went wrong, email us and we'll help.

Reminders don't show up

Check that notifications are allowed for Checkpoint in iPhone Settings → Notifications → Checkpoint, and that Focus mode isn't silencing them.
